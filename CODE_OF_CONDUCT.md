# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

KAIRO contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation a respectful, constructive experience for everyone.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and accepting actionable feedback.
- Assuming good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than people.
- Respecting privacy and keeping sensitive information confidential.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not post private or sensitive details in a public issue.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
